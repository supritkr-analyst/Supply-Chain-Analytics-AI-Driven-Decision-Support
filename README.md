# Supply-Chain-Analytics-AI-Driven-Decision-Support
This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations using data analytics, business intelligence, and AI-driven insights.

 📦 Supply Chain Analytics & AI
📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics project designed to analyze procurement, suppliers, inventory, orders, and delivery performance.

The project transforms raw supply chain data into interactive dashboards, AI-driven insights, business recommendations, and management reports to support better decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze supply chain performance
Identify supplier and delivery issues
Monitor inventory and procurement
Track important business KPIs
Identify potential supply chain risks
Generate AI-driven insights
Provide actionable business recommendations
📊 Dataset
Records: 10,000+
Domain: Supply Chain
Data includes: Orders, Customers, Suppliers, Products, Inventory, Procurement, Shipping, Delivery, Cost and Revenue

The raw data was cleaned and prepared before analysis.

🔄 Project Workflow

Raw Data → Data Preparation → Analysis → Power BI → AI Insights → Recommendations → UAT → Management Reporting

The project includes:

Executive Dashboard
Procurement Analysis
Supplier Analysis
Inventory Analysis
Operations Analysis
Key KPIs
Total Orders
Revenue
Procurement Cost
Inventory Value
On-Time Delivery %
Average Delivery Time
Delayed Orders
Supplier Performance
🤖 AI-Driven Insights

AI is used to support:

Delivery Risk Identification
Inventory Risk Identification
Demand Forecasting
Decision Support
💡 Business Recommendations

The analysis is used to recommend improvements in:

Supplier management
Procurement
Inventory planning
Delivery performance
Supply chain risk management

Each recommendation focuses on the problem, root cause, proposed action, and business impact.
